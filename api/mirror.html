What do we actually know?

Nothing yet. A camera detection is an observation at a particular point in time, not proof of where someone lives, works, or travels between observations.

Why transparency matters

Camera systems can combine observations over time. CIVIC WATCH helps visitors understand the distinction between collected data, inferred patterns, and verified conclusions. The companion surveillance registry will document agency policies, retention periods, vendor contracts, and published oversight records, with citations and review before publication.
