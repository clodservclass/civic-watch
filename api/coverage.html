Real-world registry versus simulated coverage

The surveillance registry records publicly documented agency programs and source links. It does not automatically infer a camera's orientation, sensor range, visibility, operating status, or blind spots. Future verified infrastructure maps may show broadly documented deployment areas, without operationally actionable detection boundaries.
