# CIVIC WATCH v0.6 — Technology Field Guide

## Features
- `/technology`: interactive, searchable documentation with deep links by technology slug.
- `/api/technology-guides`: read-only, static educational reference data.
- Registry records link to their technology guide.
- Guides cover ALPR/Flock-style systems, CCTV, facial recognition, drones, cell-site simulators, and social-media monitoring.

## Editorial policy
- Explain principles, data collection, limits, errors, oversight questions, lawful privacy, and safety.
- Do not provide operational instructions for sabotage, jamming, disabling, tampering with, or bypassing deployed surveillance equipment; do not track personnel in real time.
- Distinguish vendor marketing from independent evidence. The linked further-reading sources are starting points, not a claim of comprehensive verification.
- Verify specific vendor capabilities, legal claims, and jurisdiction-specific protest rights with primary sources before publication.
- Future revisions should include document revision dates, jurisdiction-specific legal review, and independent citations for each material technical claim.
