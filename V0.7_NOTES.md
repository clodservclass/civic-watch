# CIVIC WATCH v0.7 — Civil Liberties and Coverage Lab

## Delivered
- `/rights` and `/api/rights-guides`: six educational guides covering peaceful protest, public recording, device security, communications, evidence preservation, and emergency powers.
- `/coverage`: interactive MapLibre fictional coordinate-space simulation for ALPR, CCTV, aerial observation, and cell-site simulators. No geographic basemap, real installations, location data, real sensor measurements, or operational coverage gaps.
- Navigation from registry to the new pages, Docker packaging, and route tests.

## Mapping publication policy
- Registry data describes publicly documented programs; it does **not** imply a known sensor location.
- Future real-world maps may show officially documented installations or generalized administrative coverage areas, with explicit dates and sources, but never computed precise real-world detection boundaries, blind spots, or live personnel tracking.
- Fictional overlays must remain clearly labeled and technically separate from real-world locations.
- The current map is a conceptual diagram rendered using map controls; coordinates and angular dimensions are arbitrary, not measurements.

## Legal editorial policy
- Educational only; not jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
- Legal and vendor references require human source verification, review dates, and correction workflows before use in high-stakes situations.
- No public evidence intake or personally identifying submissions.
